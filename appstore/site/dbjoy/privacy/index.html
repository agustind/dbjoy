Privacy Policy

DBJoy for Mac · Effective October 7, 2026

Short version: DBJoy doesn't collect any information about you. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and the developer runs no servers that the app talks to.

What stays on your Mac

  • Connection settings (names, hosts, ports, users, databases, SSH tunnel settings), saved queries and query history are stored in DBJoy's own folder on your Mac.
  • Passwords for databases and SSH tunnels are stored in the macOS Keychain.
  • Exports and backups are written only to the files and folders you choose.

None of this is sent to the developer or anyone else. Deleting a connection in DBJoy also deletes its saved passwords.

Network connections

DBJoy connects only to the PostgreSQL and SSH servers you configure, to run the queries you ask for. Data you view or edit goes directly between your Mac and those servers. The app makes no other network requests.

Apple

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in macOS settings, Apple may give the developer anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. That's handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; DBJoy itself doesn't collect it.

Children

DBJoy is a developer tool. It doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
